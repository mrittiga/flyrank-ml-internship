## 2. Three Target Tasks

### Target 1: Technical Debugging
**Definition of done:** Identify and explain the likely root cause, resolve the error, run relevant tests, record the fix, and track resolution time and regressions.

### Target 2: Technical Learning and Interview Preparation
**Definition of done:** Produce a clear explanation, a practical example, and five questions. Achieve at least 4/5 correct without viewing answers first, then review mistakes and record the score.

### Target 3: Professional Writing and Documentation
**Definition of done:** Produce one complete LinkedIn post or project README, verify factual claims against real evidence, finalize within two revision rounds where practical, and obtain my approval before publishing.

## 3. Responsible AI Practices

- Never share passwords, API keys, or confidential information.
- Verify generated code and factual claims.
- Use AI to support learning rather than replace independent understanding.
- Keep personal decisions and final submission approval under my control.
- Measure correctness, usefulness, time saved, and errors introduced.

## 4. Initial Reflection

My most useful AI workflows are technical debugging, programming education, and professional writing. AI can improve productivity, but generated solutions can be incorrect or contain unsupported claims. I will evaluate these workflows through measurable outcomes, independent verification, and final output quality.

**Conclusion:** My goal is to use AI responsibly to improve productivity and learning while preserving my own judgment and accountability.
